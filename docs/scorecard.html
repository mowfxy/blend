Why it repeated itself

  1. For public pages, a script dealt 3 of the same 20 directions and the model picked one. Every install shares that library, so every model picks among the same 20.
  2. Away from public pages, the model "sketched three directions" from fixed menus: 11 silhouettes, 8 colour stances, 10 imagery media.
  3. Hard house rules forced one look onto everything: no gradients, shadows, mono or hairlines; at least 4 photos; at least 8 moving elements.
  4. It was web only. The old suite never mentions title bars, Electron, Tauri, SwiftUI, iOS or Android.
  5. It contradicted itself. It said taste is "not a list of banned effects" and then enforced a list of banned effects as FAILs.

Why .taste-skill kept appearing

The public-page specialist bundled a third-party toolkit's scripts without renaming them. draw.mjs writes .taste-skill/ in your project and ~/.taste-skill/history.json in your home folder. inspect.mjs writes .taste-skill/inspect/.

Kern writes only to .kern/, and its validator fails the build if another suite's name shows up in a shipped skill. You can delete the old m4labs-site/.taste-skill/ folder.

What Kern gave up

  • The headless-browser inspector that measured headline line counts and fonts at three widths. Kern checks renders through screenshots and the critique.
  • The script that searched open picture collections.
  • The numbers from the 247-site study. Kern keeps the principles that apply beyond websites.

Old suite: 103 files, 2.9 MB, about 153 KB of guidance loaded for a web build. Kern: 16 skills, about 0.18 MB, about 88 KB loaded for a web build.

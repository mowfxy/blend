Borrow a packet. Grow it. Bring some seed back.

Seven old varieties live in a card drawer by the returns desk. Take a packet on your library card, grow it in a pot or a plot, and let the best plant go to seed. Next autumn, the drawer is a little fuller.

Borrower’s card

  1. Take up to three packets a season with your Wren Street library card. Each packet holds enough for a two-metre row or six pots.
  2. Grow them however you like. A balcony counts. Write on the packet where you grew it and how it did.
  3. Let your best plant go to seed, and bring back one packet of it by the date we stamp on your card. Envelopes and labels are at the desk.
  4. It didn’t work? Bring the packet back empty and tell us why. Slugs, drought and forgetting are all useful to know.

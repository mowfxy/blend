When she doesn’t sail

The Marran stays in when the wind is above Force 7 from the south-west, or when fog brings visibility under 500 metres. The skipper decides at 06:30 and again before every sailing.

The crossing, minute by minute

Sit on the right-hand side, the starboard side, leaving Holm for the best view of the Lamb.

  1. 0Holm Pier

    Boarding opens ten minutes before. Cars first, then foot passengers.

  2. 8The Lamb

    A low rock in the Marran Sound. Grey seals haul out at low water. The skipper slows down if they are in.

  3. 15The deep water

    38 metres under the keel. The Outer Roads open to the south.

  4. 21Skerra Head

    The light flashes white every ten seconds and can be seen 12 miles out.

  5. 25Skerra Slip

    The café at the slip opens for the first boat.

Getting to the piers

Holm Pier

The number 4 bus from Holm village meets the 07:40, 10:20 and 17:00. There are 40 free parking spaces at the pier head, and toilets in the waiting room. A covered rack holds twelve bikes.

Skerra Slip

There is no bus. The village is 15 minutes’ walk up the slip road. Parking is for islanders with permits only. The slip café sells tickets for the islander card.
